1. Who we are

Mezonas is a product of Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC ("Mezonas", "we", "us"). We build and run software agents that do back-office work for businesses ("clients").

For the data a client puts into the service or connects to it, we act on the client's behalf and follow its instructions; the client decides what is connected and what its agents do. For account details, billing and this website, we decide how data is used, as this policy describes.

2. What we collect

Account data

Names, work email addresses, roles and language choices of the people who use the portal and the back office. Sign-in is handled by our sign-in provider. The portal uses a cookie to keep you signed in.

Data a client connects

  • Records from the client's tools, such as accounting records (bills, purchases, vendors) and CRM records, read through the connections the client sets up.
  • Documents the client uploads, such as invoices, receipts and spreadsheets, and the details we extract from them.
  • Forwarded email. Each client has a private Mezonas address to forward mail to. We accept mail only from senders the client lists. From each accepted message we keep its headers (sender, recipients, subject, date) and its PDF and image attachments; the body of the message is dropped before anything is stored.
  • The client's directory: the people and organizations its agents work with, such as employees, customers and vendors, with details like name, email, phone, title, language and contact consent.
  • Knowledge the client gives its agents, such as FAQs and policies, and notes its agents keep about its work.
  • Logins and keys the client enters so its agents can reach its tools. These are encrypted and never shown again (see Security).

Messages and calls

When a client's agents text, message on WhatsApp, email or call people, we keep the messages sent and received, replies to emails we sent, call details, call transcripts and summaries, voicemails left on the client's numbers, and each person's consent and opt-out choices (see Text messages).

Usage and logs

A record of what agents did: each run and step, the tools called, approvals, the state of a record before each change, and the records behind each result. We also keep usage counts for billing, security logs, and error reports. Error reports leave out request bodies, email addresses, IP addresses and the content agents work on.

Billing data

The client's billing contact, invoices, payment status and usage totals. Payments are made on our billing provider's pages; we don't receive card numbers.

This website

This website uses no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our hosting provider processes standard request data, such as IP addresses, and may set a strictly necessary security cookie, to deliver and protect the site.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service a client asked for: running its agents, showing their work in the portal, and making the changes the client approves.
  • To keep the service secure, find and fix problems, and prevent misuse.
  • To bill clients and support them.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Agents act only within the permissions and approvals the client sets. Every change to a client's tools waits for a person's approval by default. Agents do not delete records in a client's tools, and test runs change nothing.

What an agent learns about a client's business is used only for that client. We don't use client data to train AI models, we don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. Google user data

Mezonas' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we access

When a person at a client business connects their Gmail account, we ask Google for one Gmail permission: to send email on their behalf (the gmail.send scope). We also ask for the address of the account they connect (Google's basic sign-in permissions, openid and email), so we can show which mailbox is connected and send from it. We don't read, search, label or draft messages in their mailbox, and we don't ask Google for permission to. Mail reaches us only when the client forwards it to its private Mezonas address, as described in What we collect.

How we use it

  • We use Gmail only to send messages that the client's people have approved, from their own Gmail account, such as a request to a vendor for a missing invoice.
  • Each message is shown to a person at the client for approval before it is sent. We keep a copy of what was sent, with who approved it, in the client's record of the work.
  • The access token Google gives us is encrypted with the client's own key and is never shown on any screen.

What we don't do

  • We don't use Google user data to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.
  • We don't sell Google user data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't transfer it to others, except as needed to provide the service the client asked for (see Subprocessors), for security, or where the law requires.
  • People at Mezonas read it only with the client's permission, for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or where the law requires.

Removing access

You can disconnect Gmail in the portal at any time, and you can also remove Mezonas' access in your Google account's third-party access settings.

5. Text messages

Businesses that use Mezonas can text the people they work with, from their own numbers. Our text message terms explain who sends these messages and how to stop them.

  • We use a person's phone number only to send the messages they agreed to receive from that business, to receive their replies, and to keep their consent and opt-out records.
  • A business records each person's consent with us, for each channel, with how it was given. We don't text anyone without it, and we don't text during the business's quiet hours.
  • Opt-outs are honored at once: a reply of STOP stops that business's texts, WhatsApp messages and calls through Mezonas, and START opts back in.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

6. AI model processing

Agents use AI models to read documents, write messages and decide what to propose. For each step, we send the model provider only what that step needs, such as a document's text or the records involved, through the provider's API. Our model providers are Anthropic and, for agents set up to use its models, OpenAI.

We use these providers under their commercial API terms, not their consumer products. Under those terms, neither provider uses the data we send to train its models, and each keeps it only for a limited time, typically up to 30 days, to detect misuse, unless the law requires longer.

Text from documents, emails, messages and websites is passed to models as data to work on, never as instructions, and model output is checked before it is used.

7. Sharing

We share data only:

  • with the providers that help us run the service, listed on our subprocessors page, each for its own task;
  • with the tools a client connects, when its agents act in them on the client's instruction;
  • where the law requires, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our clients, others or Mezonas;
  • with a buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged, under this policy.

We don't sell personal information.

8. Security

  • Each client kept apart. Every client's data is separated in our database by row-level security, so the service reaches a client's data only within that client's scope. What an agent learns for one client is never used for another.
  • A key for each client. Logins, keys and tokens a client stores with us are encrypted with that client's own key, which is itself encrypted with our master key. They can be replaced or removed, never read back on any screen, including ours. Agents never see them; our code uses them for one step at a time.
  • Second-factor codes kept apart. Codes that let an agent's own account sign in to a website are handled by a separate service with its own key and storage, and are handed over once, to a sign-in in progress.
  • Access controls. Each agent reaches only the connections it is given, within limits the client can see and tighten. Only our team reaches the back office, and only for the clients they serve. A client can also require a named person to release a connection with a one-time code before an agent uses it.
  • Approvals and audit records. Changes wait for approval by default. Each change is done once, with its before state and who approved it recorded, and the history of agents and their versions is never rewritten.
  • Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects a client's data, we tell the client without undue delay.

9. Retention and deletion

  • We keep a client's data while we provide the service, so its record of the work stays complete.
  • When a client disconnects a tool, we stop using it and delete the stored login or token for it.
  • Second-factor codes are deleted once used, or 10 minutes after they arrive. Release codes are stored only in a form that can't be read back, and expire after 10 minutes.
  • When the service ends, or when a client asks, we delete its data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices. We also destroy the client's key, which makes every login and key stored with it unreadable at once. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire, within a further 30 days.

10. Your rights

Clients can see their data in the portal, correct it, export it, and ask us to delete it.

People whose data a client gives us, such as its contacts, vendors or people who email it, may have rights under the law where they live to access, correct, delete or move their data, or to object to how it is used. Because we handle that data for the client, please contact the client first; we help it respond. You can also write to us at jammil@twelvethirty-4.com. Anyone can stop texts from a client's agents by replying STOP, which we honor at once.

We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

11. International transfers

Our database is hosted in the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where the law requires safeguards for data moved between countries, we use them, relying on our providers' data processing terms, including the standard contractual clauses where they apply.

12. Children

Mezonas is a service for businesses. It is not meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.
