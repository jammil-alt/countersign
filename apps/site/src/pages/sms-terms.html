1. Who sends the messages

The messages come from a business you work with, such as a supplier, a contractor or a service provider. The business sends them through Mezonas, a service operated by Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC. Each business texts from its own phone number.

2. How you agree to them

  • You sign up yourself, on the business's texting sign-up page: you enter your mobile number, tick a box beside the words you agree to, and press "Text me". The box is never ticked for you.
  • The business shares the page's link with its own staff, and may email you an invitation with a link to it. Or you text START to the business's number.
  • Texting is your choice. Nothing else depends on it, email keeps working either way, and an invitation can be turned down with "Don't ask me again".
  • We keep a record of each sign-up: the words you agreed to, your number and when. We never text anyone who hasn't signed up this way. If you text a business first, that alone does not sign you up.

3. How to sign up for texts

Every business that texts through Mezonas uses the same sign-up, and so does Mezonas, texting as Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC. These are the screens a person sees, with Twelve Thirty-Four as the business.

The sign-up page

The page names the business, says that texting is optional, and asks for a mobile number. The person ticks the box, which starts unticked, and presses "Text me". Beside the box, word for word:

I agree to get text messages from Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC, sent through Mezonas, about my work with Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC: questions, updates, codes and alerts. No marketing. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Terms: https://mezonas.com/sms-terms. Privacy: https://mezonas.com/privacy.

An invitation by email

A business can email the people it works with an invitation in its name. It says what the texts are about, that texting is their choice and email keeps working either way, and gives a button to the sign-up page and a "Don't ask me again" link. One invitation and at most one reminder.

A confirmation text

Right after signing up, the person gets this text from the business's number:

Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC: you are signed up for texts from us. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Text START

Text START to +1 469 378 9525 to get texts from Mezonas (Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC).

Staff at the businesses we serve get 2FA/OTP verification codes (one-time security codes) and alerts about their work from this number.

4. What the messages are

Messages are about the work you do with the business: account notifications, requests for documents such as a missing invoice, appointment and order updates, and replies to your messages. Texts from Mezonas's own number are verification codes, work alerts and questions about your work. None of them are marketing.

Message frequency varies with the work. We don't send texts during the business's quiet hours, which are 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. in its time zone unless other hours are set for it.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

5. How to stop them

Reply STOP to opt out. We act on it at once: that business sends you no more texts, WhatsApp messages or calls through Mezonas. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE and OPTOUT work the same way. Reply START to opt back in.
