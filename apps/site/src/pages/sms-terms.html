1. Who sends the messages

The messages come from a business you work with, such as a supplier, a contractor or a service provider. The business sends them through Mezonas, a service operated by Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC. Each business texts from its own phone number.

2. How you agree to them

  • You agree with the business you work with, for example on a form, in writing, or by texting the business first and asking it to reply. You can also agree by texting START to the business's number.
  • Or you gave the business your mobile number for the work you do together, for example on an order form, an invoice or an account application. The business can then send you informational texts about that work. The first text names the business and tells you how to stop.
  • The business records how you agreed with us: what you agreed to, or where your number came from. WhatsApp messages always need your agreement, not only your number.
  • We never text anyone the business has not recorded this way. If you text a business first, that alone does not sign you up.

3. How to get texts from Mezonas

Mezonas also texts from its own number, as Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC. There are two ways to agree to these texts.

Text START

Text START to +1 469 378 9525 to get texts from Mezonas (Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC).

Staff at the businesses we serve get two kinds of texts this way: 2FA/OTP verification codes (one-time security codes) and alerts about their work. An admin at the business can also record a staff member's agreement under Contacts, with how it was given.

Give your number on a form or a document

Vendors and customers see this notice, word for word, where they give us their mobile number:

By giving us your mobile number you agree to texts from Mezonas (Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC) about your orders. Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

It appears in two places:

  • the vendor setup form
  • estimates, invoices and purchase orders, in the message at the foot of each

4. What the messages are

Messages are about the work you do with the business: account notifications, requests for documents such as a missing invoice, appointment and order updates, and replies to your messages. Texts from Mezonas's own number are verification codes and work alerts. None of them are marketing.

Message frequency varies with the work. We don't send texts during the business's quiet hours, which are 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. in its time zone unless other hours are set for it.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

5. How to stop them

Reply STOP to opt out. We act on it at once: that business sends you no more texts, WhatsApp messages or calls through Mezonas. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE and OPTOUT work the same way. Reply START to opt back in.
