1. Who sends the messages

The messages come from a business you work with, such as a supplier, a contractor or a service provider. The business sends them through Mezonas, a service operated by Twelve Thirty-Four Consulting LLC. Each business texts from its own phone number.

2. How you agree to them

  • You agree with the business you work with, for example on a form, in writing, or by texting the business first and asking it to reply.
  • The business records your agreement with us, with how you gave it. You can also agree by texting START to the business's number.
  • We never text anyone whose agreement the business has not recorded. If you text a business first, that alone does not sign you up.

3. What the messages are

Messages are about the work you do with the business: account notifications, requests for documents such as a missing invoice, appointment and order updates, and replies to your messages. They are not marketing.

Message frequency varies with the work. We don't send texts during the business's quiet hours, which are 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. in its time zone unless other hours are set for it.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

4. How to stop them

Reply STOP to opt out. We act on it at once: that business sends you no more texts, WhatsApp messages or calls through Mezonas. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE and OPTOUT work the same way. Reply START to opt back in.

5. Help

Reply HELP for help, or write to us at jammil@twelvethirty-4.com.

6. Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

7. Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Our privacy policy explains how we handle phone numbers and messages.
