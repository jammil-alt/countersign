Subprocessors

Last updated November 5, 2026

These are the providers we use to run Mezonas. Each one handles client data only for the task listed, under a contract with us. Our privacy policy explains how we handle data overall.

Our providers

Subprocessors, what each does and the data it handles
Provider What it does for us Data it handles
Cloudflare Hosts the service and this website, stores files, receives forwarded email and the email that carries agents' sign-in codes, and runs the browsers agents use to sign in to websites (when website logins are switched on). All service data in transit; documents and other files; forwarded email headers and attachments; screenshots taken during website sessions.
Neon Hosts our database, in the United States. Account data, client records and documents' extracted details, directories, messages and transcripts, logs, and the encrypted logins and keys clients store.
WorkOS Sign-in and invitations for the portal and back office. Users' names, email addresses and sign-in events.
Anthropic AI model processing for agents. The content each agent step needs, such as document text, records and messages.
OpenAI AI model processing, for agents set up to use its models. The content each agent step needs, such as document text, records and messages.
Twilio Texts, WhatsApp messages and phone calls on clients' numbers; one-time release codes by text; phone numbers that receive agents' sign-in codes. Phone numbers, message content, call details, voicemails and codes.
ElevenLabs The voice of agents on live phone calls. Call audio, transcripts and summaries, and what the agent may say on the call.
Resend Sends email for agents and one-time release codes, from clients' verified domains or ours. Recipients' email addresses and the content of the messages.
Stripe Billing, invoices and payments. Clients' billing contacts, company names, invoices, usage totals and payments.
Sentry Error tracking. Error reports, stripped of request bodies, email addresses, IP addresses and the content agents work on.

Services you connect

These are your own tools. You connect them and sign in to them yourself, and our agents act in them on your instruction, within the permissions you set. They are not our subprocessors: your own agreement with each provider covers the data you keep there.

Services clients connect and what agents do in them
Service What agents do there
Google Gmail Send email you have approved, from your own Gmail account.
Intuit QuickBooks Online Read accounting records and make the changes you approve.
Zoho CRM Read and update CRM records, notes and tasks you allow.
Podio Read and update items, comments and tasks in the apps you allow.
Webhook services, such as Zapier, Make or n8n Receive events you send us and send events you set up.
Websites you set up a login for Sign in as an agent account you create, read the pages you allow and download files.

Changes to this list

When we add or replace a subprocessor, we update this page and its date. We tell each client's admin by email at least 30 days before a new subprocessor starts handling client data, so you can tell us if you object.
