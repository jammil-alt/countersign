What we do

Agents do the work. People check it.

Mezonas is a managed service. We build agents for your business, run them for you and keep them working. You don't build anything and you don't learn a new tool.

  • {{icon mz-team}}

    Built for your business

    We start from agents we have already proven, then set each one up for your data, your vendors and the way you work. Your agents are yours alone.

  • {{icon mz-worksheet}}

    Working in your tools

    Your agents work where your work already is: QuickBooks, Gmail, Zoho CRM, Podio and others. They read the invoices and receipts you forward to your own Mezonas address, and send email from your Gmail once you approve it.

  • {{icon mz-sign}}

    You make the call

    Every change to your tools waits for your approval, with the before and after side by side. If you want a routine change to run on its own, we set that up with you, and you can switch it back at any time.

How it works

Four steps, and you stay in charge of each one.

  1. Tell us the work

    Bills, invoices, follow-ups, reports: tell us what takes your team's time. We agree what your agents will do and who approves what.

  2. Connect your tools

    You connect your own tools from your portal. Any login or key you enter there is encrypted and can't be read back, not even by us. You can disconnect at any time.

  3. We build and test

    We set up your agents and test them on your own data. Test runs never change anything in your tools.

  4. They work, you sign

    Your agents do the work. Changes wait for you in your portal, and every step they take is recorded with the records behind it.

Security

How we keep your data safe

Your agents need access to your tools. We keep that access narrow, and we show you all of it.

  • {{icon mz-table}}

    Your data is kept apart

    Each client's data is kept apart from every other client's, down to each row in our database. What your agents learn about your business is used only for you.

  • {{icon mz-settings}}

    Secrets nobody can read back

    Logins and keys you enter are encrypted with a key that belongs to your account alone. No screen shows them again, ours included, and agents never see them.

  • {{icon mz-sign}}

    Approval on by default

    Every change to your tools waits for a person unless you have agreed otherwise. Deleting is switched off, and test runs never write.

  • {{icon mz-receipt}}

    A record of every change

    Each change keeps who proposed it, who approved it, what it was before and the records behind it.

  • {{icon mz-team}}

    Each agent gets only what it needs

    An agent reaches only the tools it is given, within limits you can see and tighten on your Access page.

  • {{icon mz-lead}}

    Optional human release

    You can require a named person to release a tool before an agent uses it, with a one-time code sent to their phone or email.

The details are in our privacy policy.
