How we measure this site

We count our own visits to mezonas.com: which pages are read, for how long, which links are clicked and which site sent the reader. We do it ourselves. No analytics company or advertising network gets any of it.

What we keep, and what we don't

  • No cookies, and nothing stored in your browser.
  • No IP address and no browser identifier is stored. A visit is a scrambled value made with a random key that changes every day and is then deleted, so visits on different days can't be connected.
  • Your approximate location (country, region and city), the kind of device, and your browser and system families, never their versions.
  • Single page events are deleted after 90 days. A summary of each visit keeps its times to the hour and is deleted after 13 months. After that only daily totals are kept.

How to opt out

Because we store nothing in your browser, there is no switch on this page: it would have to store your choice. Instead, turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser. When your browser sends either one, the page sends us nothing, and we would not count it if it did.

Global Privacy Control is on by default in some browsers and is a setting or an extension in others. Do Not Track is in most browsers' privacy settings.
