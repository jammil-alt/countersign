# Logos

The mark is a table seen from above with six seats, always one color. The wordmark is Geist 500 in capitals, tracked 300, outlined in every file. Colors: slate #23415E (default), ink #151A21, paper #F4F5F3, black, white.

- `mezonas-logo.svg` — Horizontal lockup, slate. The default everywhere: site header, proposals, invoices, email signatures. Also `-ink`, `-paper` (on slate or dark grounds), `-black`, `-white` (on photos).
- `mezonas-logo-stacked.svg` — Stacked lockup for square and centered spaces. Also `-ink`, `-paper`.
- `mezonas-logo-endorsed.svg` — Horizontal lockup with "a Twelve Thirty-Four company". Footers and contracts. Also `-ink`, `-paper`.
- `mezonas-wordmark.svg` — Wordmark only, where the mark already shows nearby. Also `-ink`, `-paper`.
- `mezonas-mark.svg` — The mark, 24px and up. Also `-ink`, `-paper`, `-black`, `-white`.
- `mezonas-mark-small.svg` — Four-seat mark for under 24px. Also `-ink`, `-paper`.
- `mezonas-app-icon.svg`, `mezonas-app-icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon-180.png` — Paper mark on a slate square, radius 22%.
- `mezonas-avatar.svg`, `mezonas-avatar-light.svg` — Circle avatars for social and email profiles.
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png` (small mark), `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png` — Browser tabs and bookmarks.

Clear space: one seat length (about 15% of the mark's width) on every side. Minimum: mark 16px, horizontal lockup 96px, stacked 64px.
