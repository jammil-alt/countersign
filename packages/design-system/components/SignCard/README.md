# SignCard

The approval on Your Desk: what will change, before and after, the Receipts, who checked it, and one Sign button.

- Consumer provides: a signal `StatusPill` ("Waiting on you"), the finding as the title with its number, one line of what Sign does, before and after text, `ReceiptChip`s, the lead as a copper `PersonChip`, then "Ask a question" and "Sign".
- `surface`, `line` border, `radius-lg`, `space-6` padding. One SignCard per decision; never batch unrelated actions under one Sign.
