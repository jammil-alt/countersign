# AppHeader

52px (`header-h`) app bar for app.mezonas.com: the logo, a divider, the breadcrumb and the client's lead.

- Consumer provides: `mezonas-logo.svg` at 22px tall (the small mark alone under 360px wide), breadcrumb items starting at The Table, and the lead as a copper `PersonChip`.
- `surface` with a bottom `line` hairline; sticky at the top.
