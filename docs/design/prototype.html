Six ways an agent can start

ScheduleJob
Runs on a clock: every weekday at 8, the 1st of the month, every 2 hours. In the client's time zone.Weekly price review · month-end close · daily receipt sweep
EventSomething changed
A connected tool or document changed: a bill created, a charge posted, a document received, a price changed.From connectors and packs, each with a sample payload and filters
WebhookOutside system
Any system posts to the agent's own URL: Zapier, a job board, a form, the client's ERP.Signed, filtered, payload kept as data
ManualRun now
A person presses Run with a short form: from the agent's page, Your Desk, or the back office."Run collections for this customer"
MessageTalk to it
An email to the agent's address, a text or WhatsApp to its number, a /command in a thread, or Sloan passing a request."Here's the receipt for the Hafele charge"
Another agentHandoff
One agent's run finishes and hands its output to the next: Iris enters a bill, then Pia checks its prices.Same client only, with the run's sources passed along
