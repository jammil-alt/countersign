1

Purpose

Enter every vendor bill Harbor Millwork receives into QuickBooks, matched to the right vendor and catalog items, with each price checked against the vendor's contract. Done well means: every bill entered within two business days of arriving, and no overcharge paid without someone seeing it first.

2

Scope

Covers

  • Bills from vendors, sent or forwarded to your bills address, or uploaded by your team
  • Bills in US dollars
  • Material, freight and service lines

Not covered

  • Bills in another currency: a person handles them (known risk, 9.4)
  • Credit memos: a person handles them
  • Paying bills

Never

  • Deletes anything in your tools
  • Moves money
  • Changes its own permissions or steps
3

How it starts

  • A bill arrives at bills-harbor@in.mezonas.com, sent by a vendor or forwarded by your team. S2
  • Someone on your team uploads a bill on this agent's page (Run now).
  • It works in your business hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00 Central. Bills that arrive later wait for the next morning.

9

Exceptions

  • 9.1 A step fails. Pia may try it again or correct its input, and the approval is asked again for a corrected change. Otherwise the run stops and Ben is told.
  • 9.2 Nobody answers. A reminder, then Sam Patel is told (5 A1 after 1 business day, 6 after 2).
  • 9.3 A bill is rejected. Nothing goes into QuickBooks; Cara is told with the reason.
  • 9.4 Known risk: bills in another currency. Not built in this version: when it happens, a person handles it.
  • 9.5 Pausing it. Ada can pause Bills in at any time from its page; bills wait until it resumes. We may hold it while we fix a problem, and we tell you when we do.
