About this mock-up

Design only. The plan is docs/setup-plan.md. The data is made up and no route is called.

What it stands on (built)

  • Getting started's intake cards, "We think", Connect and Ask someone else (docs/plan.md §1.149, O1–O3).
  • Each agent's setup checklist and what it guards (builder-plan §6), and adjustable settings within bounds (§7).
  • The bundle's people roles bound per client agent, approver rules, away, the directory and its QuickBooks import.
  • Test runs, which never write; the SOP's steps, methods, models and people matrix.

What's new

  • Seats: a template names seat:bill-approver; each client fills it, and no version holds a person.
  • Five chapters with one line per agent of what's left and who it waits on.
  • The Tools card's "what it's for", account check and first look.
  • The walkthrough with choose, ask and never; try it on a real one, started from the portal.
  • Sloan across every chapter, proposing into the same cards.
  • The back office's Setup tab, Do it on a call, and the runbook.
